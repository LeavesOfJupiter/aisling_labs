Subj 01

These are interviews between Subject 01 and Dr Aisling. Some interviews were cut due to their explicit nature.

Dr Aisling: Subject 01. Good to see you. You are aware this interview-
01: Is to be conducted after my contraction of revenant syndrome, yes. You can drop the formalities Laura, you know my name.
Dr Aisling: My apologies 01, but we can't give any preference to any subjects at the time. Now tell me: what symptoms have you noticed since development of the disease?
01: Photophobia was the first, then I stopped seeing myself in mirrors... I noticed that I became more pale as well. You know I've likely been incubating for-
Dr Aisling: Just over a month, I am aware.
01: Then you know what happens next, right?
Dr Aisling: Well, it is our hope that you will be able to leave after a few months.
01: A few... [REDACTED] Laura that's [REDACTED] ridiculous! Dr Aisling: Manners, 01. We are working our hardest to get you out of here. 01: I understand, are there any other questions for today? I still don't know where I got it from. Dr Aisling: No, you are free to go.
01: Thank you doctor.

Roughly two months pass between usable interviews. During this time Dr Aisling begins to work on a cure for revenant syndrome.

Dr Aisling: Subject 01.
01: Laura.
Dr Aisling: Please call me Dr Aisling.
01: Fine. Dr Aisling. Are you here for more questions?
Dr Aisling: Yes, you've been doing a few rounds of treatment, how has that been helping?
01: Well my spine is better and my eyes no longer react horribly to the lights... kidding. Nothing so far. I have noticed I'm stronger now.
Dr Aisling: Interesting. Any other symptoms?
01: My hands are more stable. Wish that happened while we were in med school.
Dr Aisling: No need to dwell on the past. Tell me, have you had any... unusal tendencies as of late?
01: I've... started having compulsions. Mostly sorting. I reorganized the lab again and again. Did you know we have about 157 disposed vials right now?
Dr Aisling: I did not.
01: Am I going to see my family again?
Dr Aisling: I cannot say for certain yet. We are working on a cure, 01.
01: It's... almost Valentines day. Dr Aisling: I'm sorry that you're going to be busy that day.
01: Not like I got a date any of those years anyway. But... I would like to be a little less... poked at.
Dr Aisling: We are working on it. Continue to monitor your health 01. We will speak again in a month or so.
01: I will, see you soon.

Another two weeks pass. Subj 01 appears to become restless during this time, with compulsions and acts of violence getting worse.

Dr Aisling: Good evening 01
01: Aisling.
Dr Aisling: How has your health progressed as of late? You look as if you've seen better days.
01: I'm... hungry.
Dr Aisling: Yes, staff noticed. When we tried feeding you, you refused.
01: Not that kind of hunger.
Dr Aisling: Interesting. What have you been craving?
01: Something fresh. Something... different. I think.. blood? I need blood.
Dr Aisling: Interesting.
01: Laura, I'm hungry. Give me yours.
Dr Aisling: Guards!
01: [REDACTED] Laura you [REDACTED]. I'm hungry!
[Chair breaks]
Dr Aisling: Calm down.
[01 is shocked by guards]
01: [Screaming]
Dr Aisling: I'm sorry 01. But it has to be like this.
01: [Muffled]
Dr Aisling: This interview is over. Good night, Parker.
